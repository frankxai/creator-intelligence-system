# Suggested traffic is other videos, not magic

YouTube tells you **which videos** sent the viewer. That is a graph, not
a vibe.

**Agent use:** Content calendars should design sessions (A → B → C),
then inspect the Suggested card after publish. Do not invent MrBeast
“retention logs.” Use the platform’s own graph.
