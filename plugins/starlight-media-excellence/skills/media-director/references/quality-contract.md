# Starlight Premium Media Quality Contract — v0.1

## Aesthetic mechanism
- Start with a specific idea and human-viewer change in understanding. Create work that still feels particular when branding and effects are removed.
- Original authored evidence > generic visual spectacle. Show the user's real software and real constraints rather than fake dashboards, invented model stats or abstract neon swarms.
- Three directions must differ in story geometry, not merely styling. Select one and articulate why it communicates the thesis best.
- Preserve distinct brand languages: Starlight = precise evidence and systems; FrankX = human editorial intelligence; GenCreator = tangible creation and craft; Arcanea = original canon and cinematic wonder. Do not rebrand one as another.
- No synthetic-sounding superlatives or borrowed creator composition. Respect exact official logos/mascots and licensing.

## Still-first blocking criteria
- A visually readable primary anchor in 1–2 seconds; a dominant layout mass at 100px preview; text legible on actual mobile feed; intentional empty space and integrated ending.
- No unexplained footer void, top-heavy composition, generic cards for unrelated ideas, excessive borders, tiny pill-label clutter, gratuitous 3D/glow, inaccurate diagrams or evidence missing from proof shots.
- Run grayscale and effect-subtraction checks; demonstrate concept survives both. Compare desktop, 4:5, 9:16 and 16:9 on their actual artboards as applicable.
- Fix responsible dimension; no random global redesign and no motion on a failed still.

## Motion/video blocking criteria
- Movement reveals sequence, causality, changed state, navigation, tension or payoff; it is not decoration. Motion must not misrepresent interaction timing.
- Inspect frame 0, chosen mid-state, final frame and loop join where looping is requested. Verify caption sync, safe areas, keyboard/readable interaction when applicable, audio rights and peak/loudness by actual rendered playback when available.
- Real product footage captured from pinned deployment/build and approved reproducible fixture. No fake click trails or unverified product results.
- Render quality cannot be asserted from prompts, code inspection or a single preview thumbnail. If no renderer and inspection exist, mark `NOT_RENDERED` and give usable sources/specs.

## Release decision
- `PASS`: every hard gate passed on exact final rendition with source references and rights.
- `FAIL_FIXABLE`: known craft defects and an identified narrow repair.
- `HOLD`: missing identity, evidence, rights, source asset or runtime capability; no guess to substitute.
- Optional numerical scoring never overrides a hard failure. Human/founder approval is distinct from skill QA.

## Commercial test
- Exactly identify the reader/viewer job, landing page/demo destination, expected event and measurement owner. Avoid fabricated engagement, reach, conversion and cost metrics.
- Derivative variants retain the same campaign/work IDs and cite their own format decisions; collect actual results for review, not invented forecasts.
